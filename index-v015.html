Privacy and Data

How this prototype handles your information

Tasks are stored in your browser's local storage. This prototype does not send task content to a ClearCue server.

Anyone with access to this browser profile or device may be able to access locally stored tasks. Local storage is not encrypted.

Avoid entering passwords, banking information, identity numbers or other highly sensitive information.

Exported backup files contain your task information. Keep them somewhere private.

Data controls

Deleting tasks from this page does not erase exported backup files or copies stored elsewhere.
