Privacy and Data

About your information

This version stores tasks in your browser's local storage. It does not send task content to a ClearCue server.

Local storage is not encrypted. Other people with access to your browser profile or device may be able to see your tasks.

Avoid storing passwords, financial account details, identity numbers or other highly sensitive information.

Backup files contain your task information. Keep them private.

About ClearCue AI

ClearCue helps turn everyday messages and notes into organised action lists.

This release candidate uses rule-based detection. It is not connected to a trained AI model or an external AI service.

English and selected Setswana phrases are supported. Suggestions may be inaccurate, so review each task before saving.
